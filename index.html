Enchantment Forge

Gather runes, inscribe your will, and let Fate decide whether the magic binds.

Gather the Runes

Choose how many vowel-runes and consonant-runes to draw from the satchels.

Or inscribe a known set of runes
II

Inscribe the Enchantment

Every rune you inscribe is drawn from your hoard. Words of your own making are welcome.

Each rune you inscribe is consumed from your hoard.
III

The Wheel of Fate

The share of runes you bound is the chance your enchantment takes hold. Fate grants but one spin.

Seal an inscription to awaken the Wheel.

Runes are drawn from weighted satchels, vowels and consonants kept apart.
